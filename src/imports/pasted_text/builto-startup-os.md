From raw idea to
funded, distributed startup.

01 — VALIDATE
Real-world signals
Live OSINT, fake-door tests, micro-ad experiments, waitlist capture. Demand measured empirically before you write one line of code.
02 — STRATEGIZE
ICP, pricing, GTM
Ideal customer profile, go-to-market strategy, and dynamic execution roadmap — all grounded in your Stage 1 validation signals.
03 — QUANTIFY
Financial reality
Revenue simulation, cloud cost estimation, unit economics. If it doesn't pencil out, the system tells you before you build it.
